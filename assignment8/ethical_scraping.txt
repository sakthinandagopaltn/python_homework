Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?
Answer : 
    For all bots (User-agent: *), Wikipedia's robots.txt blocks /w/, /api/ and /trap/ , etc. 
    User-agent: *
    Disallow: /w/
    Disallow: /api/
    Disallow: /trap/
    Disallow: /wiki/Special:
    Disallow: /wiki/Spezial:
    Disallow: /wiki/Spesial:
    Disallow: /wiki/Special%3A
    Disallow: /wiki/Spezial%3A
    Disallow: /wiki/Spesial%3A


Are there specific rules for certain user agents?
Answer : 
    Yes. Many bots are banned entirely with "Disallow: /", including MJ12bot, Mediapartners-Google*,


Why websites use robots.txt?
Answer : 
    'robots.txt' helps website owners control the traffic to their site and avoid server overload.
    Ethical scraping involves reviewing and adhering to the rules set in robots.txt.
